__Furiten__

__Impossible ron__

__Furiten is a rule that prohibits you from winning on the discard of opponents (Ron) in 3 different situations:__

__Own discard__

__If you have already discarded any tile that could complete your hand, you remain furiten until you change your waiting tile(s).__

__Other discards__

__If you let pass a tile that can complete your hand, you remain furiten until your next draw, or until your next call.__

__After a Riichi__

__If you let pass a tile that can complete your hand once you have announced a Riichi, you remain furiten until the end of the game.__
